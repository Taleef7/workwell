## 1. What changed, and why this supersedes 2026-07-24

### 1.1 The finding: there is no FHIR-lineage Cypress, and the one attempt was abandoned

Locked decision #2 set the bar at **"import → evaluate → export → Cypress CVU+ green."** M-B built the
whole loop and it runs through the product API over a third party's archive, producing Cypress's own
expected counts exactly (ADR-055, ADR-056). Cypress then graded it **red**, and reading its source told us
why: `extract_results_by_ids` calls `find_measure_node(measure.hqmf_id, doc)` and **returns `{}`
immediately** when the document's measure identity is not the one Cypress holds. Cypress has **CMS125v14**,
the QDM lineage. We run and report **CMS125FHIR v1.0.000**, the QI-Core lineage.

Two consequences the first reading missed:

- **The 45/53 supplemental-data errors are downstream of that same short-circuit**, not an independent gap.
  `reported_sup = (reported_result[:supplemental_data] || {})[pop_key]` — when the extraction is `{}` there
  is nothing to key into. Emitting perfect RACE/ETHNICITY/SEX/PAYER today would not move the verdict by one
  error.
- **Populations are matched on `@root` carrying a per-population UUID.** The QI-Core artifact has no such
  UUIDs at all — its populations are *named* (`InitialPopulation_1`, `Numerator_1`). So there is nothing in
  our lineage to put there. This is not a labelling gap we could close from our own artifact.

**QRDA Category III is structurally an HQMF/QDM-identity format.** Its identity model has no counterpart in
the FHIR measure lineage. That is a property of the format, not a defect in our work.

Then the decisive external fact: **`projecttacoma/cvu-fhir` — MITRE's fork of Cypress, described in its own
README as "an open source tool for testing electronic Clinical Quality Measure calculation" — has 3,771
commits and was last pushed 13 April 2023.** Someone tried to build Cypress-for-FHIR and shelved it. Cypress
itself is actively maintained (v7.5.1, 30 Jul 2026) and contains **zero** mentions of FHIR, QI-Core or dQM.

So the choice was never green-versus-red. It is:

> **green on the ruler CMS is migrating away from**, or **measured against the ecosystem's own content and
> an independent engine, in the column CMS is migrating toward, where no ruler exists to be green on.**

### 1.2 The second finding: WorkWell was aiming at the wrong prize

The 2026-07-24 roadmap inherited Nicole's framing that "the real EHR proof path is QRDA-I ingest →
calculate → QRDA-I/III export → Cypress → ONC." That is correct **for an EHR seeking certification**.
WorkWell is not one, and does not need to be:

- **MIE's WebChart is already ONC-certified** on ~33/49 measures. Re-earning that in a second engine has no
  business payoff for MIE.
- Doug's actual ask was **packaging** — make the engine and its resources consumable across MIE
  (`@mieweb/*`), which is a library-and-contract problem, not a certification problem.
- The **original Vision Doc** was an occupational-health compliance agent for Enterprise Health/WebChart
  workflows, over policies that **have no official measures at all**.

Official-eCQM execution (M-A) remains essential, but its role is **credibility, not certification**: it is
how we prove the engine computes what measure developers intended, over artifacts anyone can check.

### 1.3 Where the regulation actually is (2026-08-04)

- ONC **§170.315(c)(1)–(c)(3) still name QRDA I/III only** (baseline: the 2020 CMS QRDA IGs; the 2025 IGs
  are SVAP-optional). **No FHIR alternative exists in the rule today.**
- **HTI-5** (proposed 22 Dec 2025, FR 29 Dec 2025, comments closed 27 Feb 2026) would remove **34 of 60**
  certification criteria and re-center the program on FHIR. **Not final.** (c)(4) removal is
  well-corroborated; the exact (c)(3) revision is **single-source (vendor blog) and unverified** — read the
  HTI-5 chart or the FR text before relying on it.
- CMS put **49 Eligible Clinician FHIR dQMs** (plus 17 IP, 4 OP) out for public comment 21 Jan – 25 Feb
  2026, and asked an **RFI** on FHIR-based eCQM reporting in the FY2026 IPPS rule. No mandate, **no QRDA
  sunset date**.
- **QPP accepts QPP JSON and QRDA III XML only.** Its `submissionFormat` enum is `JSON`/`XML`; there is no
  MeasureReport endpoint. Treat any "QPP accepts FHIR" claim as **false unless CMS documents it** — the
  common confusion is CMS's *proprietary* QPP JSON being called "FHIR JSON."

**Correction to the record:** the "~2030" date this project has repeated for CMS FHIR endpoints is **not
attributable to CMS**. It traces mainly to NCQA's HEDIS-digitalization goal and secondary coverage; CMS's
own pages state the goal with **no year**, and the original RFI was framed around "all dQMs by 2025," which
slipped. Say **"no published date."**

## 2. Owner decisions (locked 2026-08-04)

These supersede the correspondingly numbered items in `docs/LOCKED_DECISIONS.md` §4 where they conflict.

1. **WorkWell is supplementary to WebChart and does NOT pursue ONC certification.** WebChart carries
   certification. WorkWell carries the engine. No work is justified by "certification needs it."
2. **The verification bar is no longer "Cypress CVU+ green."** It is the **FHIR-column verification set**
   in §4 below. The QRDA I/III machinery is **kept** — it is built, it validates clean against the HL7 base
   ruler, and it is what makes WorkWell legible to an EHR audience — but a Cypress *Calculation Check*
   green is explicitly **not** a goal, because reaching it requires a QDM execution path we are not
   building.
3. **We do not relabel.** Emitting a QDM-lineage measure identity over QI-Core-executed counts stays
   forbidden (ADR-046 d3/d4, reaffirmed by ADR-058). A green obtained that way would carry **no information
   we do not already have** — #388 already measured 64/64 and 150/150 subject-level agreement against
   Cypress's own expected results — at the cost of a false provenance claim in a document that leaves the
   building.
4. **We do not build or vendor a QDM execution path** (`cqm-execution` + QDM measure content), because
   decision 1 removes the only reason to. Revisit **only** if MIE states that certification of WorkWell's
   engine is a business goal.
5. **The engine and its packaging are the primary deliverable** (M-C promoted, see §5). Doug's `@mieweb/*`
   ask and the versioned compliance API are the shape MIE consumes.
6. **The differentiator is the measures nobody publishes** (M-E). Official eCQMs prove the engine;
   occupational/OSHA content is the thing no competitor can obtain by downloading CMS artifacts.

Carried forward unchanged from 2026-07-24 §4: **packaging is a pnpm workspace under the neutral scope
`@workwell/*`**, pitched for `@mieweb/*` adoption later; **authored cms122/125 subsets retire to the
fidelity lab** post-flip (issue #377).

## 3. North star (revised)

**WorkWell = an open-source, embeddable clinical-quality engine that MIE can drop into any product, which:**

1. **Runs official eCQMs verbatim** (published MADiE/eCQI QI-Core artifacts, hash-pinned, terminology-pinned)
   over any data source — FHIR API (WebChart/teatea), WCDB SQL (shim), plain JSON bundles — **proven by
   third-party-authored test content and an independently written engine**, not by a certification tool
   that does not exist for this column.
2. **Authors, tests and verifies the measures that do not officially exist** — occupational/OSHA and
   payer-insight — published in the community `dqm-content` shape so the same tooling that consumes CMS
   content consumes MIE's.
3. **Ships as packages** (`@workwell/*` → pitched `@mieweb/*`) with a **versioned compliance API contract**,
   so the value is reusable across MIE rather than trapped in one app.
4. **Stays on the standards curve and is visibly in the room**: QI-Core/US Quality Core stamped, DEQM
   MeasureReport as the FHIR reporting resource, CQI WG participation, connectathon results on the record.

**Two-track posture (unchanged in substance, sharpened in wording):** execution and the data model live on
the FHIR column; QRDA I/III is retained as an **interoperability bridge**, not as a certification target.
QDM appears **only** as a translation at the QRDA boundary — never a QDM-native engine.

## 4. The verification model (this replaces "Cypress green")

The old bar was a single external pass/fail. The new bar is a **named set of checks, each with a stated
scope and a stated limit.** Nothing here yields a certificate; every claim must say who graded it.

| # | Check | What it grades | Independent of us? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | **MADiE test cases** (`dqm-content-qicore-2025`) | Calculation, against measure-developer-authored expected results | Content is third-party; execution is ours | **RUNNING — 410/410 exact across all EIGHT gated measures** (CMS122 55, CMS125 66, CMS2 36, CMS68 19, CMS951 55, CMS138 47, CMS130 64, CMS165 68; 0 unexpected, 0 errors). Permanent CI gate. **Gated ≠ routed** — only CMS122/CMS125 route, demo/production only. **CMS138's green is weaker than the other seven**: upstream ships its bundle one value set short, so those four codes are ours (ADR-053) |
| V2 | **Cypress C2 offline comparison** | Import → executor, against a third party's patients and answers | Oracle fully independent (QDM engine, their patients) | **DONE — 64/64 and 150/150 subjects, every population** (ADR-055) |
| V3 | **FHIR validator + DEQM STU5 package** | Our MeasureReport's structural conformance | Yes (HL7 IG package) | **RUN 2026-08-04 (B6) — base R4 **0 errors** across 4 report shapes; DEQM gap **12 (3 per report, identical)**. A GAP measurement, not a claim: we assert no DEQM `meta.profile`. `docs/evidence/DEQM_VALIDATION_2026-08-04.md` |
| V4 | **Cross-execution vs Java `cqf-fhir-cr`** | Calculation, against a second independently written engine | Yes | **RUN 2026-08-04 (B7) — **255/278 across SIX measures; CMS68/CMS951/CMS138 at 100%**. CMS122 49/55, CMS125 56/66, CMS2 29/36; IPP+DENOM agree on all 278. Terminology excluded (re-proved cold — `$evaluate-measure` CACHES). Cause **PROVEN by construction** for CMS125/CMS122: the failing conjunct is `"Has Dementia Medications"`, isolated by three single-variable mutations. CMS2's shape (`NUMER 1→0`) is undiagnosed. CMS130/165 unswept. `docs/evidence/CROSS_ENGINE_2026-08-04.md` |
| V5 | **Inferno US Quality Core Test Kit** | Our FHIR **data surface** (the WebChart ingress output) | Yes (MITRE) | **NOT STARTED** |
| V6 | **CMS7-FQR connectathon participation** | Peer verification; the de facto third-party check in this space | Yes | **CONTRIBUTION DRAFTED 2026-08-04** — 3 findings classified A/B/C/D, one proven by construction (`docs/evidence/CONNECTATHON_DISCREPANCIES_2026-08-04.md`). Submission is an owner calendar step |
| V7 | **`cql-tests` language conformance** | The CQL engine's language semantics | Yes (public report card) | Citable today: `cql-execution` 3.3.x posts **1,533 pass / 81 fail / 113 skip / 4 error** vs the Java reference engine's 1,537 |
| — | QRDA I/III vs the **HL7 base ruler** | Document structure | Yes (CVU+/Schematron/XSD) | **DONE — 0 findings both document types** (#380/#381/#384) |
| — | Cypress **Calculation Check** green | — | — | **RETIRED AS A GOAL** (owner decision 2) |

**Why V4 matters more than a Cypress green would have.** `fqm-testify` and `deqm-test-server` both wrap
**`fqm-execution`**, the library we already run — comparing against them compares our engine to itself.
The Java `cqf-fhir-cr` stack is a genuinely separate codebase (different CQL engine, different data
provider). Running CMS's test cases through both and diffing converts *"our engine agrees with our engine"*
into *"two independently written engines agree on CMS's own test cases."* That is a **stronger** claim than
the retired bar would have produced.

**Accepted cost, stated:** V4 puts a JVM back into the **verification** path, which cuts against ADR-008's
direction. Accepted deliberately as a **dev-time oracle only** — never a runtime or packaged dependency.
`cqf-tooling`'s `ExecuteMeasureTestOperation` exists but its **CLI surface is undocumented**; budget for
driving it programmatically.

## 6. Corrections to the record (carried out by this change)

1. **"~2030" for CMS FHIR endpoints is not CMS-attributable.** → "no published date." (§1.3)
2. **"QI-Core STU7 = US Core 7 = WebChart's exact surface" is half right and misleading.** QI-Core v7.0.2
   (STU7, 2026-04-04) does align to US Core 7. But **CMS's shipping measure content is authored on QI-Core
   6**, and the ecosystem's forward direction is **US Quality Core 0.5.0 over US Core 6.1.0**
   (`dqm-content-cms-2025`). We have been aiming at a version the content is not on. → M-D0.
3. **"Cypress CVU+ is the verification bar"** — in `docs/STANDARDS_CONFORMANCE.md` and the `conformance`
   skill. → replaced by §4's set. Left stale, this is a gate that quietly enforces a retired goal.

   identity short-circuit. → corrected in ADR-058 and the conformance matrix.

## 8. Risks

- **V4 may not reproduce.** Two engines disagreeing on CMS's own cases is a *result*, not a failure, but it
  costs time to classify. The connectathon's own run found 3 of 74 measures disputed, so expect some.
- **The wave-2 flip gate is unsolved** (M-A5) and is the one place where "routable" outruns "verifiable."
- **HTI-5 is not final.** If its final form keeps (c)(3) as-is, nothing changes for us; if it names a FHIR
  quality criterion, M-B's bridge posture becomes more valuable, not less. Either way decision 1 holds.
- **Connectathon cadence is external.** Slots are quarterly-ish; missing one costs a quarter.
- **Deferring supplemental data (B8)** means a future receiver requirement lands as unplanned work. Accepted
  because no receiver reads it today.
